Sound Engine Online Swagger OpenAPI

Your creative command center — compose, feel, and shape music through intent. Every knob below maps to a musical action.

🎹 Compose & Create
POST /generate
Bring your musical vision to life — describe a mood, pick instruments, set the groove, and let the engine compose harmony, melody, and arrangement.
mood harmony arrangement instruments
POST /interrogate
Ask the engine anything — "What chord comes next?", "Make it sadder", "Suggest a bridge section". Your creative conversation partner.
chat creative direction
🎭 Feeling & Emotion
GET /emotions
Browse the palette of emotions the engine understands — from tender intimacy to fierce energy. Use these to guide your compositions.
palette mood presets
POST /audio/classify
Drop in an audio clip and discover its emotional signature — what feelings does this sound carry?
analysis emotion detection
POST /audio/valence-arousal
Map any sound onto the feeling grid — how positive/negative and how energetic/calm is this audio?
valence arousal mood mapping
✍️ Lyrics & Words
GET /lyrics
Retrieve the current lyrics — your words, your story.
read
POST /lyrics
Write or paste your lyrics so the engine can weave them into the musical fabric.
writelyric sync
🎛️ Sound Shaping
GET /config/humanizer
Check the humanizer settings — the subtle timing, velocity, and feel variations that make MIDI sound like a real player.
feelgroove
PUT /config/humanizer
Dial in the human feel — adjust timing drift, velocity curves, and micro-groove to taste.
adjustfeel knobs
POST /config/humanizer/reload
Reset the humanizer to its saved preset — like hitting "recall" on a mixer.
reset
🌈 Visuals & Spectocloud
GET /spectocloud/presets
Browse visual presets for the Spectocloud — particle colors, shapes, and motion styles that paint your sound.
visual presets
POST /spectocloud/render
Render a Spectocloud visualization — see your sound as a living particle cloud.
renderparticles
🎤 Voice & Audio Models
POST /voice/classify
Identify a singer's range — soprano, alto, tenor, or bass — so the engine can write in their sweet spot.
voice typerange
GET /audio/models
See which AI listening models are loaded — emotion classifiers, timbre analyzers, and more.
AI modelsstatus
🔊 Playback
GET /audio/{file}
Stream any generated audio file — WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG.
streamplayback
🩺 System
GET /health
Quick pulse check — is the engine running?
status
